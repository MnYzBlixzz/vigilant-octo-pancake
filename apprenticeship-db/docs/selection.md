# Selection processes: what to prepare for

With A\*A\*A\* predicted, academic entry isn't the filter. These stages are. Researched November 2026 from employer
pages, Gradcracker/Higherin listings and candidate reviews. Formats change each year, so check the invitation email.

| Employer | Stages | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Babcock / Cavendish Nuclear / Devonport** | Online form → online ability tests → one-way video interview → assessment centre | Window opens October and **applications are reviewed in the order received; can close early**. Assessment centres Nov–Mar: presentation, group task, interview. Babcock says AI may help check grammar/clarity, but write your own answers. |
| **Thales** | Online form + CV → online psychometric assessment → virtual assessment centre | Assessment centre Feb–May 2027: group exercises + competency interviews. Vetting: evidence of identity, right to work, 5 years' travel/education history. |

## Preparation checklist
1. **Numerical / mechanical reasoning tests.** These will be easy for you, but they're timed. Practise for speed under time pressure.
2. **Situational judgement tests (SJTs).** Read the company's values page. SJT answers are scored against them.
3. **Video interviews.** Practise answering in 60–90 seconds to a camera. Use STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for competency questions.
4. **"Why this company / why an apprenticeship, not uni?"** Every scheme asks this. With your grades, interviewers *will* ask why not Cambridge/Imperial. Have a clear, genuine answer: earning while learning, no debt, real engineering from day one, a specific product or sector you care about.
5. **Technical curiosity.** Bring 1–2 things you've built, fixed, coded or investigated (an EPQ, a school project, F1 in Schools, Arkwright, a robotics club). Assessment centres reward enthusiasm for how things work.
6. **Group exercises.** Assessors look for listening, building on others' ideas and time-keeping, not dominance.
7. **Security vetting (defence/nuclear).** SC needs ~5 years' UK residence history. Some AWE / Rolls-Royce Submarines roles need British nationality.
