# Alternatives and backups: sponsored degrees, scholarships, gap years

A degree apprenticeship isn't the only way to get paid industry experience without big debt. With strong predicted grades,
these routes are realistic and are worth running **in parallel** with apprenticeship applications through a UCAS
application (the UCAS deadline for most courses is late January; Oxbridge was mid-October).

## 2. Scholarships to pair with a normal university degree
| Scheme | Who / value | When | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IMechE Undergraduate Scholarships** | Students starting an IMechE-accredited degree. Several named awards. | Usually opens in spring before entry; check for the 2027 round | https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/study/scholarships-funding/imeche-undergraduate-scholarships |
| **IET Future Talent Awards** (Launch / Boost / Diamond Jubilee) | Students starting IET-accredited degrees (electrical/electronic/mechatronic/systems) | Previous round closed 11 Sep, paid in January | https://www.theiet.org/impact-society/awards-scholarships/iet-future-talent-awards/ |

| **UK Semiconductor Talent Award** | Government-funded bursary for students starting relevant degrees, aimed at under-represented groups | 2026/27 closed; register interest for 2027/28 | https://ukesf.org/Semiconductor-Talent-Award |
| **Imperial Kingsbury Scholarship** | Full fees + **£12,500/yr**. Must do UK engineering industry placements. | 2026/27 listing; check for 2027/28 | https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/scholarships-search/kingsbury-scholarship-20262027.php |
| **CIBSE-linked bursaries** | Building services degrees, arranged through partner universities | Via your course | https://www.cibse.org |
| **University engineering scholarships** | Many universities offer merit scholarships for A\*A\*A, e.g. Warwick, Bath, Southampton, Bristol, Loughborough | Check each university's funding page | |

**Employer-sponsored degrees:** most big firms (Rolls-Royce, BAE, EDF, Sellafield, Arup) now put school leavers into
**degree apprenticeships** instead, and sponsor full-time students mainly through **placement years** and **summer
internships** (e.g. Sellafield, EDF, RWE, Network Rail, AWE, WSP; many 2027 placements are already advertised for current
undergraduates).

## 3. Gap year: paid industry placement before university
| Scheme | Notes | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **EDT Year in Industry** | 10–12 month **paid** STEM placement before or during university, with a mentor. The main pre-uni gap-year route. | https://www.etrust.org.uk/programmes-platinum-placements |
| **NG Bailey gap-year placement** | Paid MEP engineering work during a 12-month gap in studies | https://ngbailey.com/work-placement-year-internship |

A gap-year placement also strengthens a later apprenticeship or graduate application, and many students get
sponsored by their placement company.

## 4. Full-time degrees with paid placements
Universities with strong industrial-placement programmes in your sectors: Bath, Loughborough, Surrey, Brunel, Bristol
("with Study in Industry"), Leeds ("Industrial" BEng), Sheffield, Warwick (WMG links to JLR), Cardiff (compound
semiconductors), Southampton. Placement salaries are typically £22–27k for the year.

## How this fits the plan
1. Apply to 8–12 degree apprenticeships (core list: `SELECT * FROM my_matches;`).
2. Also submit a UCAS application, e.g. MEng Mechanical / General Engineering, choosing courses with placement years.
3. If you get both, compare total pay and debt, how relevant the work is, and the CEng route, using `docs/scoring.md`.
4. EDT Year in Industry is a good fallback if neither route works out first time.
