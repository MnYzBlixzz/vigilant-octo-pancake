# Fit Score (0–100)

Score each programme out of 100 to decide where to spend application time.
Change the weights to suit what matters most to you. They should add up to 100.

| Criterion | Weight | 0 points | Full points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interest in the sector/role | 20 | Not interested | Dream area |
| Qualification | 15 | L3/L4 only | Accredited BEng/MEng (CEng route) |
| Grade fit | 15 | `below` | `above` or `meets` |
| Location / commute | 10 | Relocation with no support | Live at home or well supported |
| Pay (year 1 + progression) | 10 | Apprentice minimum wage | Top of comparison group |
| Job on completion | 10 | `not_stated` | `guaranteed` |
| Hands-on mechanical content | 10 | Mostly desk/admin | Design + build + test |
| Employer reputation / reviews | 5 | Poor reviews | Strong reviews |
| Tie-in / downsides | 5 | Heavy repayment clause | None |

**Suggested thresholds:** ≥ 75 apply first and properly · 55–74 apply · < 55 only as a backup.

**Spread the risk:** aim for ~8–12 applications across at least three sectors. Include
2–3 schemes where your grades sit well above the requirement, and a sponsored degree
or UCAS offer as a fallback.
