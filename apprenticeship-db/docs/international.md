# Outside the UK: what a UK citizen can realistically do

Researched November 2026. Rules change, so check with the official source before relying on any of this.

| Country | Route | Can a UK citizen take the place? | Language | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🇮🇪 **Ireland** | Apprenticeships at NFQ Levels 6–8. The **Manufacturing Engineering** apprenticeship gives a Level 7 BEng in 3 years or a Level 8 BEng (Hons) in 4. Employers include Analog Devices, Intel, medtech and pharma. | **Yes, no permit needed.** The Common Travel Area gives British citizens the right to live, work and study in Ireland. | English | **Most realistic option abroad.** Semiconductor fabs (ADI Limerick, Intel Leixlip) hire apprentices. |
| 🇩🇪 **Germany** | *Duales Studium*: an employment contract plus a B.Eng. at a partner Hochschule, alternating study and work phases, 3–4 years, paid about €1,000–1,400 a month. Thousands of places at Siemens, Bosch, Infineon, BMW, Mercedes, ZF, E.ON and others. | **Yes, with a residence permit.** UK citizens can enter without a visa and apply for the permit at the local Ausländerbehörde after arriving, under § 41 AufenthV. Get it before starting work. | **German, usually B2–C1.** This is the real barrier. | Worth it only if you speak German or will spend the gap learning it. A-levels are normally accepted as university entry (check anabin / uni-assist). |
| 🇦🇹 🇨🇭 **Austria / Switzerland** | Austria: dual study similar to Germany's. Switzerland: apprenticeships, plus *praxisintegriertes Bachelorstudium* at universities of applied sciences. | Austria: residence permit needed. Switzerland: UK citizens are no longer covered by EU free movement, so a permit is needed and quotas apply. | German / French / Italian | Hard. Not included as rows. |
| 🇳🇱 **Netherlands** | BBL work-and-learn routes are vocational and taught in Dutch. ASML's "apprentice internships" require you to be enrolled at a university. | Residence permit needed | Dutch for BBL; English for internships | Not a school-leaver route. Better to study at a Dutch university such as TU/e or Fontys and intern at ASML. One reference row. |
| 🇺🇸 **USA** | Registered apprenticeships; co-op degrees at universities such as Drexel, Northeastern and Cincinnati. | Apprenticeships effectively need US work authorisation. Co-ops are open to international students on an F-1 visa through CPT, but you pay international tuition. | English | Not an apprenticeship route. Not included. |
| 🇦🇺 **Australia** | Degree apprenticeships, e.g. Flinders University with ASC for mechanical/electrical. | **No.** They are for citizens, permanent residents and New Zealanders only. | English | Not eligible. Not included. |

## How Irish levels map to UK levels

| Irish NFQ | Award | Nearest UK (RQF) |
|---|---|---|
| Level 6 | Higher Certificate / Advanced Craft Certificate | Level 4–5 (HNC/HND) |
| Level 7 | Ordinary bachelor's (BEng) | Level 6, without honours |
| Level 8 | Honours bachelor's (BEng Hons) | Level 6 |

Engineers Ireland accredits the Level 7 Manufacturing Engineering apprenticeship. If you might move back to the UK later, ask Engineers Ireland and IMechE how it counts toward UK registration (IEng/CEng).
